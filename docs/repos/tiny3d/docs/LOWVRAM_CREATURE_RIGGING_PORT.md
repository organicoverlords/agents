# LowVRAM creature rigging recovery and Tiny3D ownership

Tiny3D issue #133 consumes the preserved LowVRAM creature rigging lineages instead of rebuilding them from memory. The first recovered specimen is the historical Fennec playable rig. It is deliberately classified as a **tailed ground creature with a humanoid-compatible core**, not as proof of a true quadruped pipeline.

The exact LowVRAM Fennec implementation and receipts are vendored under `vendor/lowvram_creature_rigs/fennec/` from private preservation ref `origin/preserve/rigging-20260828/fennec-playable-rig` at commit `beb6e99874e2e12e1bd5d28cf0a5d56567562255`. `contracts/lowvram-creature-rigging-port-v1.json` hash-binds those files and records the Tiny3D ownership/disposition for skeleton fitting, bounded weights, locomotion actions, and tail secondary motion.

The original generated Fennec payloads had disappeared from their LowVRAM output paths. The exact FBX survived through P3 Git LFS as `SourceAssets/Fennec/fennec_playable_new.fbx`; its LFS OID and recovered SHA-256 are `b9aae905aa294ec826b87f0f13ec655f9487d4f1ea236b3d9b533742a43c8d4c`, exactly matching the LowVRAM receipt. A verified copy now also exists under `C:/Users/Lauri/Desktop/LOWVRAM_RIG_PRESERVATION/fennec/`.

A fresh Blender 5.2 replay recovered one 29-bone `Fennec_Skeleton`, one skinned production mesh, and the Idle/Walk/Run/Turn actions. A fresh GLB re-export is motion-measured by Tiny3D and proves one skin, 29 joints, and nonzero skeletal motion in all four actions. This is structural/motion proof only: it does **not** upgrade fresh rendered deformation quality or current P3 runtime behavior. Historical LowVRAM P3 receipts remain preserved as historical evidence, not current acceptance.

The second preserved specimen is the LowVRAM Jellyfish rig from `origin/preserve/rigging-20260828/jellyfish-tentacled-rig` at `f500d6e69899279a225956bd4b6d9b31c3abea90`. Tiny3D preserves the exact rig compiler, animation authoring/verifier code, body/rig/motion receipts, membrane mapping, and compact HoverIdle/SwimPulse visual proof loops. The reference contract is a **tentacled swimming creature**: a 220-bone radial-bell rig with 12 bell radial bones and 22 tentacle chains, max four influences, zero unweighted vertices, and zero measured cross-appendage leakage after pruning. Its required actions are HoverIdle, SwimPulse, Drift, and Turn. Primary bell/swim motion remains keyframed; only the distal 35% of tentacle chains may be physics-driven secondary motion.

The historical Jellyfish `.blend`, rig FBX, source GLB, and the four exact LowVRAM accepted animation FBXs were local-only and are currently **NOT_RECOVERED**. Their recorded identities remain preserved. A later P3 fixed animation-only generation survived in Git LFS at commit `930173f40008647d80a5a5aee1c8aa734b9f9f36`; all four FBXs were recovered to `C:/Users/Lauri/Desktop/LOWVRAM_RIG_PRESERVATION/jellyfish/p3_fixed_930173f4/` and freshly inspected in Blender 5.2. Each carries the same 220-bone `JellyfishArmature`, and their frame ranges exactly match the preserved LowVRAM motion contract: HoverIdle 72, SwimPulse 40, Drift 120, Turn 56. These P3 binaries are a distinct later lineage and do not replace or impersonate the missing historical LowVRAM exports.

Fresh rendered deformation quality and current P3 runtime acceptance remain **NOT_PROVEN** for the recovered Jellyfish binaries. The vendored historical proof loops remain historical regression evidence only.

A third preserved lineage is the historical **Warelephant quadruped candidate** from LowVRAM master-unification commit `8d22520b02995fb50cf74893c4b8bbcb6185d0e6`. Tiny3D now preserves the exact Elephant rig/QA/Unreal orchestration scripts, the surviving `fox-animations.glb` reference library (`1e01b7c2...ebf2`), and the P0 `creature_armored_siege_elephant` source image (`395bb97c...3766`). The historical rig is genuinely four-legged: it fits the fox quadruped hierarchy to target bounds, retargets every reference clip, scales hip translation by body-height ratio, and explicitly preserves the reference four-foot contact pattern.

P3 also still preserves the historical Warelephant materialization in Git LFS. The bound receipt records a static source asset plus the original skeletal mesh/skeleton and 14 clips, and a later visual-fix skeletal mesh/skeleton with the same 14 clips. Both generations include Idle, Walk, Run, and Sit. These are durable historical materialization identities, not fresh runtime acceptance.

The Elephant lineage is deliberately exposed through `build_creature_morphology_candidate(...)`, not `build_creature_morphology_plan("quadruped")`. Its historical binder accepted automatic weights at 60% weighted-vertex coverage or fell back to geodesic/proximity weights; it did **not** cap influences. Its own report contract set `visual_deformation_proven=false` and required an independent skin validator plus visual deformation review before promotion. Therefore generic quadruped remains **NOT_PROVEN** until a fresh bounded-weight, rendered-deformation, and P3 runtime proof pass succeeds.

Run `uv run --locked --python 3.11 python scripts/verify_lowvram_creature_rigging_port.py` before changing creature rigging ownership.
